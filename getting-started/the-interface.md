# The interface

Draftline has one window. Everything happens inside it, and most of it can be
put away when you only want to write.

## The title bar

Frameless, so it holds the window controls, the book's title, and the menu.
The menu is where anything that acts on the whole book lives:

| Item | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Chapter History** | Earlier saved versions of the chapter you are in |
| **Book Details & Editions** | The book's title, author, blurb and publishing record |
| **Export** | Produces files — EPUB, DOCX, PDF, reading copies |
| **Help & Documentation** | Opens these pages |
| **Report an Issue** / **Request a Feature** | Opens the issue tracker |
| **Settings** | Everything configurable |
| **Plugins** | Goes straight to the plugins screen |
| **New Window** | A second Draftline, on the start screen |
| **Back to Library** | Saves, closes the book, returns to the start screen |
| **Save & Quit** | Saves and exits |

**New Window** opens on the start screen rather than inheriting your book,
because the same book cannot be open twice — see
[Your files](/reference/your-files). Two different books side by side is fine,
and is what it is for.

## The left panel

Two tabs at its head: **Manuscript** and **Planner**.

**Manuscript** is your chapter list, in reading order, in three groups: front
matter, the body, and back matter. Click a chapter to open it. Drag to
reorder. Right-click for the rest.

**Planner** is the same panel showing story lines instead of chapters — see
[The Planner](/planning/the-planner).

The panel can be dragged narrower or wider, and the divider remembers where
you left it.

## The editor

The middle, and the point. It shows one chapter at a time on a page against a
plain ground, at a width chosen for reading rather than for filling your
monitor.

There is no ruler, no style gallery and no page-layout controls, and that is
deliberate: how the book *looks* is decided once, later, when you export an
edition — not per paragraph while you are writing. See
[The editor](/writing/the-editor).

## The tool rail

Down the right edge, a column of glyphs. Each opens a panel over the right of
the window:

- **Dashboard** — word counts, progress, and how the book is shaped
- **Characters** — the cast, and who appears where
- **Signals**, **Prose**, **Pacing**, **Chapters**, **Review** — readings of
  the manuscript as it stands
- **AI** and **AI Analysis** — only when you have turned AI on and supplied a
  key of your own

Click the same glyph again to close the panel. Nothing in the rail changes
your text unless you accept a change it offers.

## The status bar

Along the bottom: where you are, the word count, whether the book is saved,
and what Draftline is doing if it is doing something. Saving happens on its
own a few seconds after you stop typing; the status bar is where you confirm
it happened.

## Getting it all out of the way

Close the left panel and the tool rail and you have a page and a cursor. The
menu still reaches everything, and the status bar still tells you the book is
safe.
