# Characters

The **Characters** glyph in the tool rail, or the Cast view for the whole
window.

<!-- SCREENSHOT: planning/characters.png
     The characters view with several people, one selected showing its
     details and where they appear. Invented cast. -->

## What is in it

Each person has a name, whatever description and notes you want to keep, and
the chapters they appear in.

The appearances are worked out by reading the manuscript, so they follow your
writing rather than your bookkeeping. Write a character into a new scene and
they turn up there without you recording it.

## What Draftline notices by itself

It reads the manuscript for names and proposes people it thinks are in the
book, which saves typing a cast list by hand.

It is a guess and it is presented as one. You confirm or dismiss each
suggestion, and nothing becomes part of your cast because software thought so.
Expect it to be confident about a place name that looks like a surname, and to
miss someone mentioned twice in ninety thousand words.

Names invented for a novel are exactly the case where this is hardest. Correct
it and move on; the corrections stick.

## What it is not

It does not write character sheets, and it does not have opinions about your
characters. It records who is in the book and where.
