# The Planner

The **Planner** tab at the head of the left panel. It is an outliner that
lives in the book, so the plan and the prose travel together.

Nothing here is required, and nothing here writes your manuscript. The Planner
is what you would otherwise keep in a notebook.

![Planner board](/planning/planner-board.png)

## Story lines

A story line is a thread: the main plot, a romance, a mystery, a character's
arc. Each has a colour, and cards belong to one.

Lines are yours to invent. A book might have three or a dozen.

## Cards

A card is something that happens. Give it a title and a note, put it on a
line, and place it in the chapter where it belongs — or leave it unplaced
while you are still deciding.

## The two views

**Timeline** lays the book out left to right by chapter, with story lines as
rows. It answers "what is happening at this point in the book" and shows where
a thread goes quiet for nine chapters.

**Cards** groups by story line instead, so you read one thread from beginning
to end without the rest in the way. You can switch it to group by chapter from
the toolbar, though grouped that way it says much the same as the timeline.

![Planner timeline](/planning/planner-timeline.png)

## Scratchpad and synopsis

**Scratchpad** is unstructured notes attached to the book — the place for
things that are not yet a card.

**Synopsis** assembles what you have into a readable summary, which is a
quicker way to see whether the plot makes sense than reading eighty thousand
words of it.

## Importing an outline

If you planned somewhere else, paste it in. Draftline reads it into cards and
lines and shows you what it made before anything is added, so a badly parsed
outline costs you a click rather than an afternoon.
