# Spelling and grammar

Both are on by default and both can be turned off in
**[Settings](/reference/settings)**.

## Spelling

Misspelled words are underlined as you type. Right-click for suggestions, or
to add the word to your dictionary.

**Add to dictionary** is the one that matters for fiction. A novel is full of
words no dictionary has: invented names, invented places, invented things.
Add them once and they stop being underlined everywhere.

Your added words are yours, not the book's, so they follow you across every
book you write. A series keeps its vocabulary.

## Grammar

Marks likely problems: doubled words, mismatched quotation marks, spacing
that will look wrong when set.

Fiction breaks grammatical rules constantly and on purpose. Sentence
fragments. Dialogue that does not parse. A paragraph that is one word long.

So treat grammar marks as a second pair of eyes with no taste — useful for
the doubled "the the" you cannot see any more, not for whether a sentence
should stand. Ignore it freely. It keeps no score.

## What neither will do

Neither rewrites anything. Nothing changes in your manuscript unless you
change it.

If you want prose *suggestions* rather than spelling, that is AI Studio, it is
off by default, and it needs a key of your own — see
**[Settings](/reference/settings)**.
